* Overture
 The title screen song.
 Our games always have quiet title screens.
 This time, it contains hints of the Orient, 
 but is mainly a fantasy kind of song.
　

* Journey into the Night
 The BGM for Stage 1. 
 Some may be familiar with it from our previous works.
　Setting off on a journey into the ECHIDNA rampant night.
 You could say that it's Mirea's theme song.
 After doing something certain in the game, 
 if you return to the starting location, things aint pretty...


* Yggdrasil -Root of the World-
 The BGM for Stage 2. Foot of the World Tree.
 You are aiming for Yggdrasil... or not. Rather a hornet's nest.
 With forests barely penetrated by the hands of humanity, 
 it's a new frontier for residential development by monsters.


* Sacred to the Darkness
　The BGM for Stage 3. A place consecrated for non-humans.
　The great ECHIDNA stone on a nameless mountain is considered 
 a sacred place by monsters and the final battleground by humanity.
　In the last part, the enemy onslaught is pretty fierce, 
 so in sync with that the BGM grows ever more intense.


* Awaken One
　Generic Boss BGM. The Awoken Dog.
　It utilizes a 5-beat rhythm, so it should fit the creepy, 
 anxious feeling towards the 'UNKNOWN' identity of this boss.
 You could say that the base and other low tones are the focus.


* Awaken Deep Ones
　The Last Boss BGM. Why hath you not let sleeping dogs lie.
 Compared to the generic boss BGM, the intro of this BGM 
 is solemn and grave laden with a heavy dollop of doom.
 With a bit of glisten, it feels appropriate for a Last Boss BGM.


* Creators
 Ending BGM. The people that created this game.
　Thank you very much for playing to the end.
