<Overview>

 This is a Vorish Action Game.
 Play as MIREA the Valkyrie, and 3 other characters 
 and defeat monsters to return peace to the city!

 If your HP/SP is exhausted after being caught, 
 you'll be gobbled down as a tasty monster snack!



<Window Size>

- x2 Magnification. Toggle display to 1280x960 resolution.
- Full Screen. Toggle display to Full Screen

(NOTE) Full Screen may not work depending on your PC settings.
If you experience trouble with frozen screens upon resizing, 
then please try playing x2 Magnification instead of Full Screen.


<Controls>

 [Left Arrow]	: Move Left
 [Right Arrow]	: Move Right
 [Down Arrow]	: Crouch
 [Z]		: Jump (& Double Jump)
 [X]		: Attack
 [C]		: SP Attack
 [A]		: Skip Hold/Vore motions
 [S][ESC]	: Pause / Config Settings
 [D]		: Toggle Zoom ON/OFF



<Character Status>

 HP:	(Hit Points) The GREEN gauge. 
	Decreases upon receiving enemy attacks. 
	If it reaches 0, you will lose 1 Heart.
 
 SP:	(Skill Points) The BLUE gauge.
	Decreases when using Special Attacks (C key)
	If you are caught by monster, the SP gauge protects you.
	If it reaches 0, you will be devoured and the amount 
	of damage received will increase significantly.

 EXP:	(Experience) The YELLOW gauge.
	SP Attack LV will increase when this gauge fills up.

 Lv.:	(Level) Current SP Attack Lv. (Max Level = 3)
	On Lv UP --> Max HP/SP & SP Attack power increases.
	Filling up the Lv.3 EXP gauge will replenish your 
	SP gauge to MAX (only once until reset).
	(NOTE) EXP & Lv. are reset when your HP reaches 0 
	and you lose a Heart, or when you clear the stage.

Heart:	The number of lives your character has.
	If your Hearts run out, then the game is over.



<Regarding Hold/Vore motions>

 All monsters have Hold actions.

 Hold/Vore motions proceed with the Z key.
 Almost all motion can be skipped with the A key.
 During some motions, you can resist with the Movement keys.

 If your SP is exhausted when you're caught,
 your character will be swallowed whole 
 and absorbed by digestive fluids...!
